Contact Form created by Josol, Trichelle Mae M.
Course/Section: BSCpE3A

This contact form draws inspiration from high-contrast themes, which help minimize eye strain during extended screen use and improve readability.
I selected a dark background with aqua highlights and uppercase text to enhance comfort in low-light settings and achieve a modern aesthetic.

Alan Sans was chosen as the font for its balance of professionalism and approachability.
The aqua accents stand out against the deep navy background, offering a cool contrast that remains subtle.

The form is centered and outlined with double aqua borders for a tidy and balanced appearance. Placeholders are semi-transparent to avoid being distracting.
Glowing borders and button effects on hover add interactivity, while the button shifts color when pressed to indicate activation.

My goal was to create a design that is minimalistic, comfortable for the eyes, and visually engaging in dark environments.
